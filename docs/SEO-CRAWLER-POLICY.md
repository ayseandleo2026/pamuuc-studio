# Crawler policy

Three separate decisions, stated deliberately. Implemented in `robots.txt`,
generated by `tools/build.mjs`.

| Group | Bots | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search and citation | Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot | **Allow** | These feed Google, Bing, ChatGPT search and Claude search. Blocking any of them removes the studio from the results it wants to appear in. OpenAI states explicitly that OAI-SearchBot must not be blocked for ChatGPT search summaries and citations. |
| User-directed retrieval | ChatGPT-User, Claude-User | **Allow** | A person has asked an assistant to open a specific page. Refusing is refusing a reader. |
| Model training and development | GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended | **Allow** | The one genuine business choice. Allowing may increase how well the studio is represented in future models; withdrawing protects the content from training corpora. Change `Allow` to `Disallow` under this group only — search visibility is unaffected because these are different bots from the first group. |

## Rules that follow from this

- **Never use `Disallow` to keep a page out of the index.** A blocked crawler
  cannot read a `noindex` tag, so the URL stays eligible to appear as a bare
  link. This exact fault existed on the legal pages and was fixed on
  4 September 2026: the `Disallow` lines were removed and the existing
  `noindex` left to do the work.
- **Anything genuinely private must never be on the public site**, regardless
  of crawler policy. Client materials, proposals and working files stay out.
- **Re-check the official documentation before changing the allowlist.**
  OpenAI and Anthropic revise bot names and IP ranges, and neither offers a
  webmaster console that confirms inclusion.

## Snippet policy

Indexable pages send
`index,follow,max-image-preview:large,max-snippet:-1,max-video-preview:-1`.
`max-snippet:-1` places no length limit on the snippet, which is what keeps
the pages eligible for generative results. Do not add `nosnippet` or a numeric
`max-snippet` without accepting that cost.
